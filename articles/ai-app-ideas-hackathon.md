## Why you should participate in an AI Hackathon

Do you have an idea for an app and want to create a working prototype in just 7 days? Or do you have an idea, but don't know people from different fields of expertise to take your project further? Or maybe you don't have an idea and want to improve your skills by building and improving others' visions? If you answered yes to any of those questions, AI hackathons are events designed for you.

Taking part in an AI hackathon is an opportunity to collaborate with like-minded enthusiasts, learn new skills, and explore what AI technologies such as Cohere and ChatGPT offer. These events foster personal growth and innovation. So, what apps can you build during an AI hackathon? Here are five ideas you can bring to life using the Cohere and ChatGPT APIs.

## What Can You Build with Cohere and ChatGPT APIs?

Cohere is an AI lab that provides access to Large Language Models and NLP tools through one API. Among their products, you can find Generate, Embed, Semantic Search, or Classify.

ChatGPT, a product of OpenAI, hardly needs an introduction: many of us have seen screenshots of conversations with it, read stories, or heard songs in the style of different artists written by it. But it can do more than SEO, scripts for YouTube videos, or writing business emails.

Let's look at what you can build with Cohere and ChatGPT.

### AI-assisted mental health companion

Create an app that uses the ChatGPT API to deliver personalized, empathetic, and supportive conversations to users struggling with mental health issues. This AI companion could offer a safe space for individuals to share their thoughts and feelings, receive encouragement, and access helpful resources. Because the topic is sensitive, make clear in the app that it is not a substitute for professional help.

### Smart language learning assistant

Use the Cohere API to build a language learning tool that tailors its approach based on the user's proficiency and learning style. This app could provide context-aware translations, grammar explanations, and interactive language exercises to help users learn foreign languages.

### AI-powered job interview coach

Develop an app that uses the ChatGPT API to simulate job interviews, allowing users to practice their responses to various interview questions. The app could analyze users' answers, provide constructive feedback, and offer suggestions to improve their performance in real interviews.

### Personalized news and content curator

Combine the Cohere and ChatGPT APIs to create an app that curates news articles, blog posts, and other content based on the user's interests, reading habits, and preferences. This app could also generate AI-written summaries of the selected content, allowing users to stay informed without spending hours reading.

### AI-Driven Creativity Booster

Design an app that uses the ChatGPT API to help users overcome writer's block, brainstorm ideas, or add a touch of creativity to their work. Whether it's coming up with catchy headlines, generating story ideas, or providing creative inspiration, this app could be a muse for writers and artists alike.
