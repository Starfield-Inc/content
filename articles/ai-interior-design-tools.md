## AI-tailored solution for home decor

Are you tired of spending hours trying to find the perfect color scheme for your living room? Or struggling to visualize how furniture will fit into your space? In this article, we will explore 7 AI-powered interior design tools that can transform your home decorating experience. These tools use artificial intelligence to simplify and enhance the design process, making it easier to create personalized spaces.

## The Advancement of AI in Interior Design

Before we dive into the specific tools, let's take a moment to appreciate the advancements that AI has brought to the world of interior design. With the help of AI algorithms, interior designers and homeowners alike can now access a wide range of features and functionalities that were previously unimaginable. From generating realistic 3D visualizations to suggesting furniture arrangements, AI has changed the way we approach interior design. Or even by leveraging the 2D potential of those interior design apps, every user can test different versions of his dreamed space, before taking the decision on which version he will go.

Let’s look at the tools.

### Color palette inspiration with Colormind.io

[Colormind.io](http://colormind.io/) generates color schemes, using deep-learning algorithms to extract harmonious color palettes from photographs, films, and popular art. With a fresh batch of datasets uploaded daily, you can expect new inspiration each time you return. Colormind.io goes beyond simply identifying the most representative hues, diligently curating cohesive color combinations that bring your interior design visions to life.

Handy interior design AI tools to help find perfect colors for your own home. Or workplace. Or any place where you want to have a perfect color match!

Is it free? **Yes!**

### Room makeovers with Remodeled.ai

[Remodeled.ai](https://remodeled.ai/) is an AI-powered interior design tool tailored to help you breathe new life into your rooms with ease. Leveraging advanced algorithms, the app meticulously examines your room's dimensions, layout, and furnishings, subsequently generating bespoke room designs that flawlessly align with your chosen theme.

Here's how you can effortlessly remodel your space:

1. Upload a snapshot of your room.

2. Indicate the room type you wish to revamp.

3. Select your desired design theme.

4. Within moments, see a reimagined room generated by the AI model.

A tool to see how this particular space would look in different interior design styles, or even how it looked like if you changed it into for example gym or coffee shop!

Is it free? **No, but you get free 3 generations to try it out. **

### 2D and 3D home design with Planner 5D

[Planner 5D](https://planner5d.com/) is AI-powered home design software. By providing tools such as AI floor plan recognition, intelligent furniture placement suggestions, and real-time rendering, Planner5D.com streamlines the design process.

Embrace a rich suite of features for 2D/3D floor plans and interior designs, including the Design Generator, Smart Wizard (AI-powered tool), VR (Virtual Reality), and AR (Augmented Reality) technologies, all built to offer project visualization. With Planner 5D, create customized furniture, modify colors, and adjust finishings to suit your unique decor preferences, all while previewing the evolving designs.

Accessible on a wide range of platforms including Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android, Planner 5D supports a home design workflow on each of them. You can even hire through their website a professional interior designer if you’re stuck!

Is it free? **Yes!**

### Design ideas with CoolAIid

[CoolAIid](https://coolaiid.com/) is an AI-powered website that offers tailored interior design ideas for both DIY users and professional designers. The platform presents a wide assortment of styles, catering to varying room categories and furnishing your home with diverse elements, including rugs, gallery walls, and decorative moldings.

Utilize the Pro Tools offered by CoolAIid for control over aspects of your virtual home staging. By drawing directly (inpainting) onto the provided images, these features allow you to interact with various design elements. CoolAIid also translates your sketches into realistic furniture pieces.

You can use CoolAIid to craft personalized spaces in your home.

Is it free? **No, but you have 30 AI renders for free and access to some AI PRO tools features.**

### Virtual staging with Dreamhouse AI

[Dreamhouse AI](https://dreamhouseai.com/) is a website that combines artificial intelligence with interior design and virtual staging to bring your dream home to life. By uploading a photo of your room, Dreamhouse AI redesigns it, so you can test many styles and themes before committing to the final build.

Perfect for homeowners looking to revitalize their rooms, real estate agents seeking virtual staging, and interior designers or architects aiming to visualize concepts and convey designs to clients.

It also supports outpainting: you choose furniture you don’t like and ask the AI to generate something else in its place.

Is it free? **No, but you have 5 credits for free to start (but with free plan you can’t upload your own images to change)**

### Architectural design with AITWO.CO

[AITWO.CO](https://aitwo.co/) is an AI-powered design platform for creating houses and buildings. Initiate your design journey by selecting a building type, style, and color palette, then then customize it with AI.

Provide tailored instructions to the AI, dictating elements such as lighting, composition, symmetry, intricacies, shapes, and perspectives. The platform combines your creative input with AI generation.

Upon verifying your email, the site offers five free design generations.

Is it free? **No, but you have 5 credits for free to start.**

### Design ideas and shopping links with Spacely AI

[Spacely AI](https://www.spacely.ai/) is a website that uses artificial intelligence to deliver interior design ideas for bedrooms, living rooms, and kitchens. The site states that it has generated over 310,000 design concepts for over 35,000 homeowners and interior designers.

Explore a vast array of styles, including minimalist, Japanese, and Ryokan, to create a distinct home aesthetic. Spacely AI also has an AI tool that identifies furniture pieces within your chosen designs and locates links to purchase similar items online.

Is it free? **Yes!**

### Bonus: Japanese-style interiors with Jife

[Jife](https://jife.com/) is an AI-driven website that restyles your rooms in Japanese-inspired designs. Simply upload a photo of your space, and Jife's AI will reimagine it in a variety of Japanese styles.

Choose from a selection of room types, encompassing living rooms, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, dining rooms, toilets, and genkans. Further, personalize the experience by selecting your favorite iconic designer or traditional Japanese aesthetic, and Jife will generate designs in your chosen style.

Is it free? **No.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Can AI interior design tools truly rival human designers?

Though AI-enhanced tools can improve the design process, they cannot fully replace the creativity and expertise of human designers. AI serves as a valuable aid in decision-making for designers and homeowners, but the significance of human intuition prevails.

### Are AI-driven interior design apps costly?

The pricing of AI-powered interior design apps varies, reflecting their range of features and functionality. Many apps offer free and premium versions, catering to individual budgets and requirements.

### Can beginners use AI-assisted interior design tools?

Yes! AI-powered interior design tools cater to users of all skill levels, thanks to their user-friendly nature. Professionals and novices alike can benefit from these tools to realize their design aspirations.
