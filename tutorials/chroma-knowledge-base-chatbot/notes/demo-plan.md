# Demo plan

The demo lasts three minutes.

1. Joel states the problem in 30 seconds with one photo of a flooded mooring.
2. Tomas opens the map, picks Port Merrin and sets an alert level of 3.2 metres.
3. Maya triggers a test forecast. A text message arrives on Joel's phone during the demo.
4. Priya shows one chart: predicted against measured water level for last winter.
5. Joel closes with what is next: more harbours and a way to check accuracy for each one.

A backup video of the whole demo is saved on two laptops.
