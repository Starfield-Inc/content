# Known risks

- The SMS sandbox only texts verified numbers, so the live demo will text Joel's phone and Maya's phone only.
- The surge model has only been checked on one harbour, Port Merrin. Its error on the other three harbours is unknown.
- Weather forecasts can arrive late. If the feed is more than 6 hours old, the app shows a warning instead of an alert.
- Venue wifi may be slow. Priya keeps a copy of all data on a laptop.
