The Harbour Street library reopened on Monday after a nine-week renovation. The work replaced the roof, which had leaked for several winters, and moved the children's section from the basement to the ground floor. Staff say the move was the most requested change in a survey they ran two years ago.

The reading room now has 60 seats, up from 38, and a new quiet area at the back has desks with power sockets. The library has also added a small workshop room that can be booked for free by local groups. Early bookings include a knitting circle and a coding club for teenagers.

Opening hours change as well. The library will open until 8 pm on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and on Saturday it will open at 9 am instead of 10 am. The council paid for the work from its maintenance budget, and the library says it expects no change to its yearly running costs.

Some users are less happy. The car park beside the building is closed until spring while a drainage fault is repaired, and the nearest alternative is a ten-minute walk away. The library has asked the council for a temporary drop-off bay and is waiting for an answer.
